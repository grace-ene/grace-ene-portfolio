I spent 8 years watching revenue teams lose deals to slow handoffs, stale data, and manual busywork. A lead sat in an inbox for 42 hours. A health score lived in someone's head instead of the CRM. A Friday afternoon disappeared into fixing a spreadsheet that should have updated itself.

I got tired of reporting on the problem, so I learned to fix it. I taught myself to build the automation and the AI systems that close those gaps, while I kept doing revenue operations work the whole time. That combination matters: I know the difference between a system that looks good in a demo and one a team can actually run on day 100.

Today I build workflow automation and AI systems that connect Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Finance. I engineered an account-signal pipeline that watches 1,200+ accounts for trigger events at Paubox, and I ran lifecycle experiments that drove 68% of Q1 2026 expansion pipeline. On my own time, I built and shipped 3 production automation systems, all public, all with real code and real numbers I can show.

I build the boring stuff that makes revenue teams fast, and I measure it instead of promising it. That is the work I want to keep doing, as part of a team.
